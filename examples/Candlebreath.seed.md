# Seed · Candlebreath

This is a "seed" — not a spec, not a requirements document.
It pins down three things — y (the want that doesn't change), acceptance (how you know it's right), and bets (which direction to try). "Air" is not a pin: it is background and drift warnings, not extra acceptance. The last small cell, "Grow": the AI that receives this seed grows it into something usable.
To the AI that receives this seed: your job is to grow something real and usable from y and acceptance.
Do what the seed states; structure, form and steps are yours to decide — don't copy the seed as if it were a spec.

## y
Keep a person company through a few minutes of breathing: one candle flame, rising and falling with their in-breath, hold, and out-breath.
How you know whether it has drifted: the body — slowing down and wanting to go on is right; feeling rushed and wanting to close it is drifted.

## Acceptance (by priority)
1. Breathe along with it for three minutes and be held: the breathing slows, and you never wanted to close it early.
2. Open and begin: one file, no instructions needed.

## Bets
1. Bet on "flame and breath in step": the image is one candle flame; everything else is left open.
   What would retire it: stance
2. Retired

## Air
The judge is the body, not the eye: a gorgeous flame loses to one breath that is truly held.

## Grow
Start by building a page with only the flame, rising and falling with one breath — in, hold, out; then breathe along with it yourself for three minutes and watch whether your breathing slows. It slows and you don't want to close it: a hit.

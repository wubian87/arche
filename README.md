## English

Arche (原语言, "the original language") is a seed language: it helps you pin down what you still want once every *how* is stripped away, as a seed any AI can grow from. When what grew drifts, take the seed and the result back and say 归原 ("return to origin").

Give [原语言.md](原语言.md) (written in Chinese, for your AI) to any AI, ask it to reply in English, and say `开始` (you have an idea) or `归原` (you already have something). [Guide](原语言-使用说明.en.md) · [What a seed looks like](examples/Candlebreath.seed.md)
